Honest limitations

  • Every future class counts equally here. A double-period, a lab, or a class your college weights differently will shift the real number.
  • Targets vary by institution, course, and sometimes by subject. BunkCalc does not know your college's rule — confirm it before acting on a bunk count.
  • Medical or duty-leave rules, condonation, and late attendance updates are not modelled. If your portal disagrees with your notes, trust the portal.
